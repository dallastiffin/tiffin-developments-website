Common questions

What does the $299 include?

All three systems: the AI chatbot, the auto-text conversation bot and the AI voice agent, set up and managed for you. We go over setup on your free call.

Do I need my brokerage’s permission?

Some brokerages have rules about tools that message clients for you, so it’s worth a quick check. We set everything up to clearly name you and your brokerage.

Will clients know they’re talking to AI?

We can have it introduce itself as your virtual assistant. Most people just want fast, useful answers and a time that works.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. Calls forward from your existing number, so your signs, cards and listings stay the same.

What happens when I want to take over?

Jump into any text conversation from your phone and the bot steps back. You choose which calls ring straight through to you.
