Happy clients mean to leave a review, then forget

Closing day is busy and emotional. Your clients are happy, but leaving a review is the last thing on their mind.

Asking by hand is easy to forget, and agents feel awkward asking twice. Months later, your Google profile still shows a handful of reviews.

An automated system asks every client at the right time, follows up once, and keeps a steady flow of fresh reviews on your profile.

What you get

Triggered by your closings

One-tap review link

One polite reminder

Private feedback option

How it works

  1. Connect your profile

    We link your Google Business Profile and set up your review link.

Common questions

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. Asking real clients for honest reviews is fine. We never buy reviews, filter who gets asked or offer gifts in exchange, since those break Google’s rules.

Can I ask past clients too?

Yes. We can send a one-time request to past clients who have given consent to hear from you.

What if I get a bad review?

You get an alert right away and a calm, professional draft reply. You decide what gets posted.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

Yes. If you don’t have one yet, we can help you set it up properly first.
