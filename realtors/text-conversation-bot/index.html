The agent who replies first usually gets the client

Online buyers and sellers often reach out to more than one agent at a time. The first agent to reply starts the relationship.

But you’re showing homes, at the lawyer’s office or with your family. Replying in a few hours is normal, and by then they’re talking to someone else.

A text bot replies in seconds, every time. It also follows up for weeks or months, so the lead who’s “just looking” now is still yours when they’re ready.

Common questions

Is texting leads allowed in Canada?

Yes, when the person contacted you first or gave consent. Every flow includes clear identification and an easy way to opt out, in line with Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL).

Will it text from my number?

It can text from your existing business number or a new local number. Either way, the conversation shows up on your phone.

Can it work with my CRM?

Yes. It can run inside GoHighLevel or connect to Follow Up Boss, Lofty and other CRMs.

What about my old leads?

We can run a careful re-engagement campaign to past inquiries that gave consent, to bring back people who are now ready to move.
