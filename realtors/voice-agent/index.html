The calls you miss are often your best leads

Sign calls and referral calls come in while you’re showing homes, presenting offers or driving. Voicemail feels like a dead end, so most callers hang up.

An assistant or answering service costs a lot and often just takes a message.

An AI voice agent answers every call on the first ring, knows your listings and books the next step with you.

  1. Map your calls

Common questions

Will callers know it’s AI?

Some will. We can have it say up front that it’s your virtual assistant. Most callers just want quick answers and a showing time.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. We forward calls from your existing number, so your signs, cards and listings stay the same.

What about offers or urgent calls?

You choose which calls ring straight through to you, like other agents calling about offers, past clients or anything marked urgent.

How long does setup take?

Most voice agents are live within one to two weeks, including testing with your listings.
