Common questions

Does it work on my website?

Almost always. It installs with a small snippet on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, most brokerage-provided agent sites and IDX sites that allow custom code.

Can it answer questions about MLS listings?

It answers from the listing details we give it, such as your own active listings. It doesn’t pull live MLS data unless your website’s IDX tool shares it.

What if someone asks for advice?

It tells them that’s a great question for you and offers to book a time. It never gives opinions on pricing, offers or the market.

Will it work with my CRM?

Yes. We can send leads to Follow Up Boss, Lofty, GoHighLevel, a Google Sheet or email.
