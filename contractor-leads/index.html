Questions

What counts as a lead?

A phone call or a form request from someone asking about your trade in your area. Spam, sales calls and wrong numbers do not count.

Are the leads really exclusive?

Yes. Each website sends its leads to one contractor. We do not sell the same lead twice.

What does it cost?

Exclusive leads are a flat $599 a month plus HST per site. The price is the same for every trade and every town.

When do I start paying?

On a new site, billing starts the day your first real lead arrives. Until then you pay nothing. On a site that is already bringing in leads, billing starts when you sign up.

Is there a contract?

No. There is no term. You pay month to month and can cancel any time.

Do you guarantee rankings or a number of leads?

No. Nobody can promise what Google will do next month. What we can do is show you what a site has brought in so far, before you decide.

How do the leads reach me?

Calls forward to your phone. Form requests go to your email right away.

What is the difference between renting and owning?

With exclusive leads, we own the site and you pay monthly for the leads. With ownership, you pay once, the site is yours, and you cover its running costs, like the domain and the phone number.

What if my trade and town are already taken?

Then we tell you. We can look at the next town over, or build a new site for you.

Where do you work?

We are based in Brantford, Ontario. Most of our sites are in Ontario and Alberta, and we can build for other parts of Canada.
