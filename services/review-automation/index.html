Happy customers forget to review

Reviews are one of the biggest reasons a business gets picked from the map results. But most satisfied customers never leave one unless they’re asked.

Asking by hand is easy to forget on a busy day. Replying gets pushed off too, and unanswered reviews make a business look absent.

Automation fixes both. The request goes out after every job, and replies are drafted for you in minutes.

What you get

Automatic requests
A text and email after each completed job or appointment, with a direct link to your review page.
One polite reminder
A single follow-up for people who didn’t get to it. Never more than that.
Private feedback option
Customers can send feedback straight to you, so you can fix a problem quickly.
Drafted replies
Every new review gets a reply written in your voice, ready to approve or post automatically.
Instant alerts
You hear about new reviews right away, especially the ones that need attention.
Monthly snapshot
Review count, rating trend and reply rate in one short report.

How it works

  1. Connect your profile

    We link your Google Business Profile and set up your review link.

  2. Choose the trigger

    A job marked done, an invoice paid or a row added to a sheet. Whatever you already do.

  3. Write the ask

    Short messages that sound like you, timed for when customers are happiest.

  4. Launch and watch

    We track requests sent, reviews earned and replies posted, then fine-tune.

A good fit for

  • Local businesses that win work from Google Maps
  • Businesses with great work and too few reviews
  • Owners who never find time to reply to reviews
  • Businesses with more than one location

Common questions

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. Asking real customers for honest reviews is fine. We never buy reviews, filter who gets asked or offer rewards, since those break Google’s rules.

What about a bad review?

You get an alert right away and a calm, professional draft reply. You decide what gets posted.

What software do I need?

None new. We trigger requests from what you already use: your calendar, invoicing tool, CRM or a Google Sheet.
