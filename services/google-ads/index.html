Google Ads

Be there the moment someone searches for what you do. There are two kinds of Google ads. We help you pick the right one, or run both.

Book a free call

Two ways to advertise on Google

Pick one or run both. We’ll recommend the right mix on your free call.

Search Ads

You pay per click

Text ads at the top of Google results for the searches you choose.

Good for
Any business, any service, any area.
You control
Keywords, areas, ad text, schedule and daily budget.
Where it sends people
Your website or a landing page built for the ad.

Local Services Ads

You pay per lead

Ads shown above regular search ads, with a Google verified badge, your reviews and a call button.

Good for
Trades, home services and some professional services in Google’s eligible categories.
You pay for
Calls and messages from customers, not clicks. You can dispute leads that don’t fit.
What’s needed
Google runs a background, licence and insurance check before ads go live. We walk you through it.

What you get

  • Keyword and area planTarget the searches that turn into paying jobs.
  • Ad setupAds, extensions, call buttons and conversion tracking.
  • Local Services Ads profileCategories, service areas, hours and verification help.
  • Lead reviewWe check lead quality and dispute bad Local Services leads.
  • Weekly tuningCut wasted clicks and add the searches that work.
  • Monthly reportCalls, forms and cost per lead in plain language.

Good fit for

  • Plumbers, HVAC, roofers and other trades
  • Cleaners, movers and landscapers
  • Lawyers, accountants and other professionals
  • Any business people search for

Questions

Which one should I start with?

If your category qualifies, Local Services Ads are often the place to start because you pay per lead. Search Ads cover everything else. We’ll tell you on the free call.

Can I run both?

Yes. Many businesses do: Local Services Ads at the very top, Search Ads just below.

How much does it cost?

Your ad budget is up to you. We go over budgets and our fee on your free call.
