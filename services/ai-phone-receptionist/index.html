Missed calls are missed customers

When you’re with a customer, on a job or driving, you can’t pick up. Most callers won’t leave a voicemail. They call the next business on the list.

After-hours calls are worse. The person calling at 9 p.m. wants help now, and whoever answers first usually gets the work.

A traditional answering service costs a lot per call and still only takes a message. An AI receptionist answers, books and follows your rules.

What you get

Answers around the clock

Sounds like a person

Asks your questions

How it works

  1. Map your calls

    We go through your services, common questions, pricing rules and what counts as urgent.

  2. Build and train

    We write the script, connect your calendar and number, and test it on real call types.

Common questions

Will callers know it’s AI?

Some will, and most won’t mind as long as it’s quick and useful. We can have it say up front that it’s a virtual assistant if you prefer.

Can I keep my phone number?

Yes. We set up call forwarding from your existing number, so nothing changes for your customers.

What if it doesn’t know an answer?

It takes the details and says you’ll follow up. It never makes up prices or promises, and the question shows up in your summary.

How long does setup take?

Most receptionists are live within one to two weeks, including testing.
