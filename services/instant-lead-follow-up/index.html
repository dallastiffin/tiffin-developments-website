The fastest reply usually gets the job

A new lead is hottest in the first few minutes. They’ve just reached out to you and probably two or three others.

Most small businesses reply hours later, or the next day. By then the customer has booked someone else.

Follow-up is where the most money gets left behind. A quote goes out, the customer goes quiet and nobody checks back.

Common questions

Is texting customers allowed?

Yes, when they’ve contacted you first or opted in. Every flow we build includes consent and an easy opt-out, in line with Canada’s anti-spam law (CASL).

Will it sound like a bot?

No. Messages are short and written the way you talk. Anything tricky gets handed to you.

Do I need a new CRM?

No. We can log leads to a Google Sheet or connect the CRM you already use.

Does it work with the AI receptionist?

Yes. Calls get answered, and anything that slips through gets a text back.
