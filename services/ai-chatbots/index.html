A modern AI chatbot reads your actual services and policies, answers in plain language and knows when to hand the conversation to you.

Common questions

Does it work on my website?

Almost always. It installs with a small snippet on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, custom sites and more.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

We limit it to your approved information and test it before launch. When it isn’t sure, it says so and takes the visitor’s details for you.

Where do the leads go?

Wherever you want: text, email, a Google Sheet or your CRM.

Can it work on Facebook or Instagram messages too?

Yes, the same assistant can answer messages on your social pages.
