Traffic isn’t the same as customers

A lot of small business websites look fine and still don’t bring in work. They try to say everything on one page, bury the phone number and give visitors no clear next step.

Buying shared leads from directories isn’t much better. The same person gets sold to four competitors, and you end up in a race to the bottom on price.

A funnel is simpler: a focused page for each service, a profile that shows up locally, and tracking that tells you which of them actually produce jobs.

What you get

Service landing pages
One focused page per service and area, written to answer what customers ask and move them to call or request a quote.
Google Business Profile setup
A complete, well-organized profile with the right categories, services, photos and posting routine.
Call and form tracking
Tracked numbers and form tags show which page, ad or profile each lead came from.
Local ads when they pay off
Google Local Services and search ads, pointed at pages built to convert, with a budget you set.
Leads that are yours
Every inquiry goes straight to you. We don’t sell or share your leads with anyone else.
Monthly numbers
A plain report: calls, forms, cost per lead and what we’re changing next.

How it works

  1. Pick the jobs you want

    We start with the services and areas that earn you the most, not a generic list.

  2. Build the pages

    Landing pages, profile updates and forms, written in plain language.

  3. Turn on tracking

Common questions

Do I need a new website?

Not always. We can add landing pages alongside your current site, or build a simple new one if the old one is holding you back.

How long before leads come in?

Ads can produce calls within days. Organic results from Google Maps and search usually build over two to four months.

Are the leads shared with other businesses?

No. Everything we build sends leads to you only.

Do I have to run ads?

No. Many funnels run on Google Business Profile and organic search alone. Ads are an option when the math works.
