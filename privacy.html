Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Tiffin Developments ("we", "us") is a sole proprietorship based in Ontario, Canada. We help small businesses get and answer more leads through lead-generation funnels, Facebook and Instagram ads, Google Search Ads and Local Services Ads, email campaigns, website chatbots and AI text-back, and AI automation. This policy explains what personal information we collect through tiffindevelopments.com and our phone numbers, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: the details you enter, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, city, the service you are interested in and any message you write.
  • Website chat: the messages you send through the chat window on our site, and any contact details you share there.
  • Calls and texts: when you call or text one of our phone numbers, including our demo lines, we receive your phone number and the content of the call or text. Calls to our demo lines are answered by an AI voice agent and may be recorded and transcribed.
  • Booking: the name, email address and any notes you give when you book a call through our calendar.
  • Basic analytics and cookies: technical information such as your browser, device, pages viewed and the site that referred you. Our hosting provider and the tools embedded on our pages (chat, calendar and fonts) may use cookies or similar technology to work properly and to count visits.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your questions and messages.
  • To book and hold calls with you.
  • To provide the services you ask us for, and to follow up about them.
  • To keep our website and tools working and to understand, in general terms, how people use the site.

We collect only what we need for these purposes.

Texts and emails (CASL)

We follow Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). We text or email you only when you have contacted us first or have given us consent. Every message identifies who it is from and gives you an easy way to opt out. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

Tools and service providers we use

We use trusted third-party services to run our business. They process information on our behalf and only for the purposes above:

  • GoHighLevel / LeadConnector: website chat, AI voice agent and text messaging, and our customer records (CRM).
  • Google: appointment booking (Google Calendar), storing contact form submissions and sending us notifications (Google Sheets, Apps Script and Workspace email), web fonts (Google Fonts), and analytics if enabled.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting for this website, which keeps standard server logs.

Some of these providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

Google account data

Our internal tool "Tiffin Sites Search Console" connects only to our own Google account to add our own websites to Google Search Console. It does not access any other person's Google data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We don’t sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law, and then delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email dallas@tiffindevelopments.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also call (226) 602-0654.

Governing law

We handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you are not satisfied with our response to a privacy concern, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
