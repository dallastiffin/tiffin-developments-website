Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Tiffin Developments ("we") runs websites that connect homeowners with local trade contractors. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you fill in a contact or estimate form, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, address or town, and a description of your project. When you call one of our phone numbers, the call may be recorded and the caller's number logged.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request and to pass it to the contractor who serves your area. We do not sell your information for any other purpose.

Google account data

Our internal tool "Tiffin Sites Search Console" connects only to our own Google account to add our own websites to Google Search Console. It does not access any other person's Google data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Form and call details are stored in our Google Workspace and call-tracking accounts and shared only with the contractor handling your request and the service providers that run these systems.

Your choices
