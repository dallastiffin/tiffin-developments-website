Admin eats your evenings

Copying a lead into a spreadsheet. Retyping it into a quote. Emailing the quote. Chasing the invoice. Every step is small, but together they eat hours every week.

Most of this work follows the same pattern every time. That makes it perfect for automation.

Modern AI can also handle the messy parts, like reading an email, pulling out the details and deciding where it goes.

Quote drafting

Invoicing and payment chasing

How it works

  1. Workflow audit

    We walk through a normal week with you and list every repeat task and where it lives.

  2. Pick the quick wins

    We start with the tasks that save the most time for the least change.

  3. Build and connect

    We link your tools, like Google Workspace, QuickBooks, Jobber or your CRM, and test each flow.

  4. Hand over and support

    You get a simple guide to what runs and when. We monitor and fix issues as they come up.

Common questions

What tools do you work with?

Most common small business tools: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, Jobber, Housecall Pro, HubSpot, Stripe and more. If it has a way to connect, we can usually automate it.

Do I need to switch software?

No. We build around what you already use. We only suggest a change if it clearly saves you time or money.

Is my data safe?

We use the official connections each tool provides, keep access limited to what each workflow needs and set everything up in accounts you own.

What if something breaks?

We monitor the workflows and get alerts when a step fails, so most issues are fixed before you notice.
