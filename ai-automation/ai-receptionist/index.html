Missed calls are missed jobs

When you're on a roof, under a sink or driving between sites, you can't pick up. Most callers won't leave a voicemail. They call the next business on the list.

After-hours calls are worse. The person with a leak at 9 p.m. wants help now. Whoever answers first usually gets the work.

A human answering service costs a lot per call and still just takes a message. An AI receptionist does more for less.

What you get

Answers 24/7

Picks up every call on the first ring, including nights, weekends and holidays. No hold music, no voicemail.

Sounds like a person

A clear, friendly voice trained on your services, service area, hours and pricing guidelines.

Qualifies the caller

Asks the questions you would ask: what they need, where they are, how soon and how to reach them.

Books the appointment

Checks your calendar and books estimates or service calls into open slots, then confirms by text.

Transfers when it matters

Hot leads and emergencies can be passed straight to your cell. You set the rules.

Sends you a summary

Every call ends with a text or email summary, plus a recording and transcript if you want them.

How it works

  1. Call map

    We go through your services, common questions, pricing rules and what counts as urgent.

  2. Build and train

    We write the agent's script, connect your calendar and phone number, and test it on real call types.

  3. Go live

    Calls forward to the agent when you don't pick up, after hours, or all the time. Your choice.

  4. Tune

    We review calls in the first weeks and tighten the answers until it handles them the way you would.

Common questions

Will callers know it's AI?

Some will, most won't care. The agent is polite, quick and useful. We can have it say up front that it's a virtual assistant if you prefer.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. We set up call forwarding from your existing number, so nothing changes for your customers.

What if a caller asks something it doesn't know?

It takes the details and tells them you'll follow up. It never makes up prices or promises. You get the question in your summary.

Can it transfer calls to me?

Yes. You decide which calls get transferred live, like emergencies or jobs over a set size, and which get booked or logged.

How long does setup take?

Most receptionists are live within one to two weeks, including testing.
