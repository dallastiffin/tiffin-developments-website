The fastest reply wins the job

A new lead is hottest in the first few minutes. They've just reached out to you and probably two or three others. The first business to reply sets the tone.

Most small businesses reply hours later, or the next day. By then the customer has booked someone else or lost interest.

Follow-up is where most money is left on the table. A quote goes out, the customer goes quiet and nobody checks back.

Common questions

Is texting customers allowed?

Yes, when they've contacted you first or opted in. We build every flow with consent and an easy opt-out, in line with Canadian anti-spam rules.

Will it sound like a bot?

No. Messages are short and written the way you talk. The AI answers simple questions and hands anything tricky to you.

Do I need a new CRM?

No. We can log leads to a Google Sheet or connect to the CRM you already use.

Can it work with the AI receptionist?

Yes. The two work well together. Calls get answered, and anything that slips through gets a text back.
